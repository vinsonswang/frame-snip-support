Save a photo from a video

Open a clip with Choose a Video or More → Choose Video from Files. Select a suggested frame or tap Browse Nearby Frames. Use Save Photo to add the current frame to Photos.

Finish setting up AI search

Open Find by Description and start the one-time download of about 255 MB. You can close the search panel and come back to check progress while Frame Snip is open. If paused, return to the panel and resume. After the download completes, search works offline with videos available on the iPhone.

Restore Pro

With the Apple Account used for the purchase, tap the crown in Frame Snip, then tap Restore Purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase.

从视频保存照片

点「选择视频」，或打开「更多」→「从文件选择视频」。选中一张推荐画面，或点「查看附近帧」逐帧查看。点「保存照片」即可保存当前帧。

完成 AI 查找准备

打开「按描述查找」，开始约 255 MB 的一次性下载。保持 Frame Snip 打开时，可以关掉查找面板，稍后再进来查看进度。暂停后可回到面板继续下载。下载完成后，本机可用的视频可以离线查找。

恢复 Pro

使用购买时的 Apple 账户，点 Frame Snip 的皇冠，再点「恢复购买」。Pro 为一次性购买。
