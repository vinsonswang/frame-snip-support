Privacy Policy

Effective date 2026-11-12

Sheng Wang provides Frame Snip, an iPhone app for saving still photos and making collages. Frame Snip works without an app account.

Your media and searches

Frame Snip opens the videos and photos you select. It may keep a temporary copy of imported media on the iPhone while it processes the file. Frame selection, collage creation and AI searches run on your iPhone. Frame Snip does not send your videos, photos, extracted frames or search descriptions to the developer.

Saving a photo or collage asks for permission to add to your Photos library. You can change this permission in Settings. Photos and videos stored in iCloud are handled by Apple according to your device settings.

Optional AI download

The first AI search requires a download of about 255 MB. The app requests the files from the download service over HTTPS. The service receives connection data, such as the requesting IP address, to deliver the files. The download server is hosted by Tencent Cloud. It keeps access logs containing the IP address, request time, request and response details, and browser or app identifiers sent with the request, such as the user-agent string. We use the logs to diagnose download issues and operate and protect the service. The server rotates these logs daily and keeps the current log and the 14 most recent rotated logs.

Frame Snip saves the AI resources on the iPhone. After setup, you can search videos available on the device offline.

Purchases

Apple handles payment and restoration for Frame Snip Pro through the App Store. Frame Snip uses StoreKit to check access to the purchased features. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app.

Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you share to answer and follow up on your request. We retain these messages for as long as needed to resolve the issue and handle related follow-up.

Manage your data

Delete App in iPhone Settings to remove Frame Snip’s app container, including its local preferences and downloaded AI resources. Manage exported photos and collages in the Photos app. For privacy questions or a request about data we may hold, email hitszwangsheng@gmail.com.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

隐私政策

生效日期 2026-11-12

Frame Snip 由 Sheng Wang 提供，用于在 iPhone 上从视频选帧和制作拼图。使用 Frame Snip 无需注册 App 账号。

照片、视频和查找描述

Frame Snip 会打开你选中的照片与视频，处理期间可能在本机保存临时副本。选帧、制作拼图和 AI 查找都在本机完成。Frame Snip 不会把你的视频、照片、截取的画面或查找描述发送给开发者。

保存照片或拼图时，App 会申请向系统相册添加照片的权限。你可以在「设置」中更改权限。保存在 iCloud 中的照片和视频由 Apple 按你的设备设置处理。

可选的 AI 下载

首次使用 AI 查找需下载约 255 MB 的资源。App 通过 HTTPS 向下载服务请求文件。服务会接收用于传输文件的连接数据，例如请求的 IP 地址。下载服务托管在腾讯云。服务器的访问日志会记录 IP 地址、请求时间、请求和响应详情，以及随请求发送的客户端信息，例如 User-Agent 字符串。我们用这些日志排查下载问题、运行和保护服务。服务器每天轮转日志，保留当前日志和最近 14 份轮转日志。

AI 资源保存在本机。下载完成后，可以离线查找本机可用的视频。

购买

Frame Snip Pro 通过 App Store 完成付款和恢复购买。Frame Snip 使用 StoreKit 检查你能否使用已购买的功能。开发者不会通过 App 收到你的银行卡信息。

支持请求

如果你通过电子邮件联系我们，我们会使用你的邮箱地址和你提供的信息回复并跟进请求，并按处理问题及后续沟通所需的时间保留邮件。

管理数据

在 iPhone「设置」中选择「删除 App」，可删除 Frame Snip 的本机容器，包括偏好设置和已下载的 AI 资源。已导出的照片和拼图可在系统相册中管理。如有隐私问题或希望查询我们可能持有的数据，请联系 hitszwangsheng@gmail.com。

本网站

这些页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。详见 GitHub 隐私声明。
